PathCopy Advanced

Steps

  1. Click an edge to use as the path. Connected edges are followed automatically — no welding needed. Click a face to use its outline. Ctrl+click (Option on macOS) adds another path; pre-selecting edges before starting the tool works too.
  2. Click the group or component to copy. Ctrl/Option+click adds more objects and the copies alternate between them.
  3. Change anything in the settings panel — the copies rebuild in place and the whole session stays a single undo step.

Keys

↑ ↓

Right-click
The same options as the panel, if the panel is closed.

Distribution modes

  • Spacing — a fixed distance; the leftover is handled by the alignment.
  • Count — exactly N copies over the whole length.
  • Fit spacing — the nearest spacing to the one you typed that divides the path evenly.
  • Fit per segment — a copy on every vertex, with the spacing fitted inside each segment. For railings and fences.
  • Vertices — one copy per vertex.

Across the path

The Across button in the panel (or a 90° extra rotation) turns the object's X axis perpendicular to the line — balusters face across the rail rather than along it.

Railing

A profile swept along the same path: rectangular or round, aligned on the centre of the line or wholly outward or inward. Raise lifts it to the top of the balusters, Extend the ends runs it past the end points. Corners are mitred, so a turn is a single piece.

Turning a copy by hand

Click a placed copy to turn it by the given angle, Shift+click turns it back. The turn survives any change of settings; clear it with the button in the panel.
